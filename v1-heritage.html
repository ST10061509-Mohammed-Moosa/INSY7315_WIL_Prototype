A message from our founder

“Buhle” means beauty. We look for it in every child we meet.

Thee Buhle Foundation started with a simple frustration: bright learners in our community were falling behind for reasons that had nothing to do with ability. No quiet place to study. No one to ask when a maths problem stopped making sense. No adult who could say “I have walked this road, and you can too.”

So we built what was missing — Saturday tutoring, mentors who show up every week, workshops that teach the things school does not, and camps where a child can simply be a child. Community. Compassion. Change. In that order, always.

Buhle

Founder & Director · Thee Buhle Foundation

Story of the year

42% to a bursary offer in two years

Thandi joined in Grade 10 with a 42% maths mark and a plan to leave school after matric. Her mentor, an engineer at a local plant, kept showing up. So did she — 34 of 38 Saturdays in her final year.

She wrote her NSC with a 71% in mathematics and holds a conditional bursary offer to study civil engineering. She now tutors Grade 9s on the Saturdays she is home.
